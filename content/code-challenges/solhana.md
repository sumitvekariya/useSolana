Solhana is a series of three progressive challenges that explore different types of vulnerabilities in Solana smart contracts. Each level increases in complexity, introducing new security concepts and exploitation techniques.

## Challenge Goals

- Learn about various vulnerability classes in Solana programs
- Understand how different attack vectors can be exploited
- Progress through increasing levels of security complexity
- Develop a comprehensive understanding of Solana security principles

Originally presented at Paradigm CTF, this challenge series offers a structured approach to learning Solana security, making it ideal for developers who want to systematically improve their understanding of smart contract vulnerabilities and exploitation techniques.
