Darksols presents a series of Solana security puzzles that progressively test your understanding of smart contract vulnerabilities and exploitation techniques. The challenge is designed to simulate complex security scenarios you might encounter in production environments.

## Challenge Goals

- Identify and exploit complex vulnerabilities in Solana contracts
- Understand advanced program security concepts
- Learn about intricate account relationships and validation
- Develop sophisticated exploit chains for smart contract vulnerabilities

Originally presented at justCTF 2022, this challenge provides a comprehensive test of your Solana security knowledge, requiring both analytical thinking and creative exploitation approaches to overcome its intentionally vulnerable smart contracts.
